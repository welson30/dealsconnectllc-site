Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how Deals Connect LLC (“we”, “us”) handles information on dealsconnectllc.com (the “Site”). It covers this Site only. Our brands, My30A Host and PaidPrime, run their own websites and apps, each with its own privacy policy.

Information we collect

  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if it appears in the message, and whatever you write. We use it to reply to you.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, the Site’s hosting and network providers automatically process technical data such as IP address, browser type, device, the pages requested, and the date and time. This is used to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the Site.

The Site has no accounts, forms, or comments, and we never ask for payment information here.

Cookies and tracking

The Site does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not track you across other websites. If that changes, we will update this page first.

Service providers

We rely on a few providers to run the Site. Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

  • Vercel hosts the Site.
  • Cloudflare registers our domain and manages its DNS.
  • Google Fonts supplies the Site’s typefaces. When a page loads, your browser requests the fonts from Google, so Google receives your IP address and browser details.
  • Google (Gmail) receives and stores the emails you send us.

How we use information

We use information to answer your messages, to operate and secure the Site, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Links to other sites

The Site links to our brands’ websites and may link to others. Their privacy practices are theirs, and their own policies apply once you leave this Site.

How long we keep information

We keep emails for as long as needed to handle your request and for ordinary business and legal records. Technical logs are kept by our providers on their own schedules.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Economic Area), you may have additional rights under local law, and we will respond as that law requires.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we handle. No method of transmission or storage on the internet is perfectly secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
